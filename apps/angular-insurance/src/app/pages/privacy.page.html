Why some things have no switch

Some processing is required to run your policy, and some is required of us by law — verifying your identity, and regulatory reporting. Those are stated rather than offered, because a switch that cannot change the outcome would be misleading.

Changing your mind

Any choice can be withdrawn at any time and it takes effect immediately. Each decision is recorded with the time you made it and the policy version in force, so the history of what you agreed to stays intact.
